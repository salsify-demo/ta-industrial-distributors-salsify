Full-line industrial distribution, Wisconsin and beyond

Full line. Local service. Content that keeps up.

T&A Industrial is a veteran-owned, full-line industrial distributor serving Wisconsin and the Upper Peninsula from Brookfield and Green Bay, with tooling, abrasives, safety, MRO, chemicals and raw metals. A lean team carries a catalog built from hundreds of supplier lines. This page is a short look at how Salsify helps a team that size compete online.

Why it matters now

Small team, big catalog

A full-line distributor carries as many SKUs as a national one, with a fraction of the staff. Supplier content still arrives in hundreds of formats and still has to be normalized before it is searchable. The difference Salsify makes is that the work becomes mapping and exceptions instead of re-keying, so a small team can keep a large catalog current.

Supplier intake

Hundreds of lines, each sending spreadsheets and PDFs in its own structure.

Online plus counter

Customers research online and buy through the rep or the counter. The content has to be right in both places.

Service content

Repair, calibration and tool service are part of the offer and need to show up alongside the products.
